# Provider Validation Protocol

The public market has multiple providers below the $35 fulfillment ceiling, but no provider is considered production-approved until they pass a paid or platform-backed sample order.

## Approval gate
A provider must satisfy all of the following:
- total delivered cost <= $35 including required file formats and one revision
- delivery <= 72 hours after complete brief
- print-ready PDF plus web-ready PNG/JPG
- commercial-use rights appropriate for client delivery
- no obvious template plagiarism, stock-license violations, or watermarks
- quality score >= 85/100 on layout, typography, hierarchy, brand fit, and export quality
- communication/reliability score >= 85/100

## Ranking policy
1. Prefer high-review-count Upwork candidates for primary fulfillment despite slightly higher starting prices.
2. Keep low-cost Fiverr candidates as backups until their quality is proven.
3. Never accept a client order if the currently available provider quote exceeds $35.
4. Never silently absorb rush fees, extra pages, extra concepts, copywriting, or additional revision costs. Reprice or reject before payment.
5. Maintain at least two approved providers before enabling unattended payment collection.
